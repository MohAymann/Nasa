The 5 Spaceflight Hazards & The Telemedicine Gap

Why traditional ground-supported medical monitoring fails on deep-space journeys

⚠️ Ground Telemedicine Blackout

Communication delays between Mars and Earth reach up to 22 minutes one-way (44-minute round trip). Real-time surgical or emergency consultation is physically impossible.

🦴 Altered Gravity Adaptation

Microgravity causes cephalad fluid shifts, baroreceptor resetting, 1–2% monthly bone density loss, and severe cardiovascular deconditioning without daily targeted countermeasures.

⚡ Ionizing Space Radiation

Unshielded galactic cosmic rays (GCR) and solar particle events (SPE) induce DNA strand breaks, elevating lifetime fatal cancer risk and cognitive degradation.

🧠 Confinement & Closed Environment

Circadian disruption (16 sunrises/day on ISS), noise, and social isolation impair sleep architecture, cognitive alertness, and crew behavioral health.

Key Feature 02

Physiological Trends & Cumulative Radiation Tracker

Long-term trajectory tracking to detect subtle microgravity degradation

📈 Dynamic Recharts Analytics

• Multi-timespan filtering: 7 Days, 14 Days, 30 Days, or All Time.
• Clinical reference bands: Dashed nominal threshold indicators.
• Telemetry statistics: Automatic computation of Minimum, Maximum, Average, and trend direction (↑, ↓, →).
• System categorization: Filter by cardiovascular, respiratory, metabolic, behavioral, or radiation.

⚡ Cumulative Dosimeter Meter

• Career Threshold Comparison: Real-time progress bar evaluating total absorbed mission dose against NASA's 1,000 mSv career limit.
• Solar Particle Event (SPE) Detection: Immediate warnings to move to module storm shelters when solar fluxes surge.
• Scientific Basis: NASA-STD-3001 and Canadian Space Agency (CSA) radiation guidelines.

Key Feature 03

Evidence-Based Clinical Protocols & Countermeasures

Actionable interventions derived from NASA's Human Research Program

📋 Dual-Tab Metric Protocols

Every indicator features:
• Action Protocol: Step-by-step guidance for Low, Normal, and High values.
• Flight Surgeon Thresholds: Explicit criteria for when crew must escalate to ground medical teams.
• Pathophysiology: Explains how microgravity alters normal bodily baseline values.

🛡️ Standard Spaceflight Directives

• ARED Exercise (2.5h/day): High-load squats & cycle intervals to stop bone loss.
• Renal & Bone Nutrition: Sodium restriction and calcium/Vit-D3 supplementation.
• Circadian Entrainment: Solid-state light shifting and acoustic suppression.
• Emergency CAPCOM: Acute stabilization checklists during comm blackouts.
